Support

Quick answers for Trekonaut. If you still need help, email us.

What is Trekonaut?

An iPhone app for hiking challenges with friends, sponsored trail drops, summit keepers, and verified arrivals. You race to a pin, prove you got there, and keep score honestly.

How does arrival verification work?

You need to be inside the challenge or drop radius with good GPS accuracy, stay there for about twenty seconds, then take a summit snap in the app. Simulated or impossible locations are blocked outside debug builds.

Do sponsored drops pay money inside the app?

No. Claiming a drop creates a proof for the sponsor. Sponsors pay rewards outside Trekonaut — for example in-store or by their own process. There are no in-app purchases for drop payouts, no entry fees, and no wagers.

Who can see my location and photos?

Verification data stays on your device unless you share with friends you invite via CloudKit. Nothing is public by default. See the privacy policy for detail.

How do friend invites work?

Invites use Apple’s CloudKit sharing. Only people you add to your circle can see shared content. You need to be signed into iCloud on your iPhone.

Why doesn’t my arrival count?

Common causes: GPS accuracy worse than about ±20 m, standing outside the pin radius, leaving before the dwell timer finishes, or skipping the in-app summit snap. Try open sky and wait for a stable fix.

When will Trekonaut be on the App Store?

We’re preparing the listing. The download button on the home page is a placeholder until the store link is live.

Is trekonaut.com live?

Not yet. This GitHub Pages site is the temporary home. The domain trekonaut.com is planned for later.

Support

Common questions from the trail.

Do I need cell service at the top?

No. Check-in uses GPS and the photo on your phone. You do not need a signal to finish verification. Sharing with friends needs iCloud when you are back online.

What if my GPS is bad?

Arrival needs you inside the pin radius with accuracy around 20 meters or better, then about 20 seconds standing still. Move to open sky and wait for a stable fix before you try again.

How do I get a gold prize?

Be first to the drop, check in, and claim it. That creates a proof for the sponsor. The sponsor sends the prize to you outside the app, by their own process.

Does it cost money?

No. It never costs anything to play. There are no entry fees and no in-app purchases for prizes.

Who can see where I hike?

Your location stays on your phone. Check-ins only go to friends you invite, through iCloud. Nothing is public by default. See Privacy.

How do I delete my data?

Clear local data from Profile in the app. Shared circle data follows Apple iCloud sharing controls for people you invited. Email us if you need help finishing a wipe.

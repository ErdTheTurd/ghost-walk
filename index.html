Summit challenges

Pin a peak. Invite your circle. First verified arrival wins.

Name the challenge, set a radius and deadline, and race friends to the spot. A live leaderboard shows who checked in.

Sponsored drops

Trail rewards from sponsors — paid outside the app.

Find a drop on the map, walk there, and claim it. Claiming creates a proof the sponsor uses to pay out (in-store, email, or their own system).

No money moves inside Trekonaut. No entry fees. No wagers.

Summit keepers

Log the peak. Keep the season.

Verified visits add up. The keeper is whoever has the most — ties go to the earliest first-of-season. Leave a short note if you want.

Arrival proof

You have to be there.

Stay inside the pin radius with solid GPS for about twenty seconds, then take a summit snap in-app. Spoofed locations don’t count.

Location and photos stay on your phone for verification. Sharing is opt-in with people you invite.

Private by default

Your map. Your circle.

Nothing goes public unless you share it. Friend invites use Apple’s CloudKit sharing — only people you add. No ads. No tracking SDKs. We don’t sell your data.

How a challenge works

  1. 1

    Pick a summit on the map and decide how close you have to get.

  2. 2

    Send it to your friends. Everyone has until the deadline.

  3. 3

    At the top, stand still for about 20 seconds and take a photo in the app. Your GPS and the photo prove you were really there.

Gold drops

Some peaks have a sponsor drop waiting at the top. The first person to get there and check in claims it, and the sponsor sends the prize to you directly. It never costs anything to play.

Summit keepers

Every peak has a keeper: the person who has checked in there the most this season. Go take it from them.

Private by default

Your location stays on your phone. Check-ins only go to friends you invite, through iCloud. There are no ads, and nobody is buying your data.

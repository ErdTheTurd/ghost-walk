Privacy

Updated November 14, 2026. Short and plain.

On your phone

Trekonaut uses GPS and an in-app summit photo to verify that you reached a peak. That check runs on your device. Location used for verification stays on your phone unless you share a check-in with friends.

Friends only

When you invite people, sharing goes through Apple iCloud (CloudKit). Only friends you invite can see what you share. There is no public feed of where you hike.

What we do not do

There are no ads. There are no tracking SDKs for ads or analytics. Nobody is buying your data. We do not sell personal information.

Gold prizes

If you claim a sponsor drop, the app builds a claim proof. The sponsor sends the prize to you outside Trekonaut. No money for prizes moves inside the app.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@trekonaut.com.

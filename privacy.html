Privacy

Last updated November 14, 2026. This policy describes how Trekonaut (iOS, bundle ID com.erd.ghostwalk) handles your information.

Short version

Location and summit photos are used on your device to verify arrivals. Friend sharing uses Apple CloudKit and only reaches people you invite. We don’t run ads, don’t use third-party tracking SDKs, and don’t sell your data.

What Trekonaut does

Trekonaut is a hiking and adventure app for iPhone. You can create summit challenges with friends, find sponsored drops, log peaks and summit keepers, and verify that you arrived at a place.

Location

When you grant location access (while using the app), Trekonaut uses your GPS position to:

  • Show nearby challenges, drops, and peaks on the map
  • Check that you are inside a pin’s radius with acceptable accuracy
  • Require a short dwell time before an arrival counts
  • Reject clearly spoofed or impossible movement when those checks are on

Location used for verification stays on your device unless you choose to share related challenge or claim data with friends you invite.

Photos (summit snaps)

Arrival claims can include a photo taken in-app (not imported from your library). That image is used to build a claim proof (including a photo hash). Photos for verification are processed on-device; they are not uploaded for advertising or analytics.

Friend sharing (CloudKit)

If you invite friends, Trekonaut uses Apple CloudKit sharing in a private circle you control. Share permissions are not public. Only people you invite can see shared circle content. Pins and local data remain on-device until you explicitly share.

Apple’s handling of iCloud / CloudKit data is covered by Apple’s privacy policy and your Apple ID settings.

Sponsored drops and rewards

Claiming a sponsored drop creates a claim proof for the sponsor. Sponsors pay rewards outside Trekonaut (for example in-store or by email). No money moves inside the app. We do not process payments for drop rewards.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising networks
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs for ads
  • No sale of personal data
  • No public feed of your location or photos

Device integrity

Future builds may use Apple App Attest (or similar) to strengthen claim proofs. That attestation stays tied to verifying arrivals — not to advertising profiles.

Children

Trekonaut is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the date above and post the new version on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@trekonaut.com. Future site: trekonaut.com.
